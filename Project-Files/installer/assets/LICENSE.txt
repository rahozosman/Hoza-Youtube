WHAT THIS SOFTWARE IS FOR

Hoza YT downloads media that a page makes openly available, to the computer it
is installed on. It is intended for media you are authorised to download.

You are responsible for the use you make of it, including compliance with the
terms of the sites you visit and with the copyright law that applies to you.


WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO

  - It does not bypass DRM or encrypted media.
  - It does not bypass paywalls, sign-in walls or other access controls.
  - It does not create qualities a source does not offer.


WHAT IT INSTALLS

  - The Hoza YT local engine, which runs only on this computer.
  - The media tools the engine needs.
  - A browser connection (Chrome native messaging), registered for the
    signed-in user only.

The engine listens on this computer's loopback address and is reachable only
from this computer. It starts when your browser needs it and stops when your
browser closes.


YOUR DATA

Settings, download history and the local database are stored on this computer.
Nothing is uploaded, and the application contains no analytics, no third-party
scripts and no remote code.
